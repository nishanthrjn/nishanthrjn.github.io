## Final image prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: 2 by 2 texture atlas of four separate portfolio project thumbnail illustrations, square canvas, four equal square quadrants with NO gaps or visible dividers. Each quadrant has a pure near-black background fading to black at its own edges, atmospheric refined blue and champagne-gold illumination, same premium cinematic 3D technical illustration style. Top left: an elegant translucent human head profile facing left with a luminous blue-and-gold neural brain and delicate connected nodes. Top right: an isometric engineering CAD model with layered floor plans, mechanical geometry, thin blueprint lines and restrained golden details. Bottom left: connected glassy enterprise database cylinders and small translucent dashboard screens, blue technical architecture and gold data connections. Bottom right: an electronic circuit board with precision components and glowing blue signal traces with tiny warm gold nodes. Each subject centered within its own quadrant, surrounded by generous dark negative space. No text, no letters, no logos, no labels, no UI borders, no watermark. Four distinct illustrations, not one connected scene. Dark and understated, highlights limited to the subjects. High resolution.

The SVG diagrams are authored from the existing project descriptions and TalentBot's local routing/retrieval implementation. They are simplified conceptual diagrams.
